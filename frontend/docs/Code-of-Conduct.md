# Code of Conduct

_The latest version is always available at [hotosm.org/code-of-conduct](https://www.hotosm.org/code-of-conduct)._

Welcome to the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT) community! We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of race, gender identity, sexual orientation, ability, appearance, background, nationality, age, religion, or beliefs.

---

## Community Principles

- **Be friendly and patient.**  
  Offer and accept feedback with kindness. Critique should be constructive, respectful, and focused on goals, not personal preferences.

- **Be welcoming.**  
  Use inclusive language and support people of all backgrounds and identities. Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

- **Be considerate.**  
  Your work affects others. Think about the impact of your decisions on users and colleagues.

- **Be respectful.**  
  Disagreement is natural, but never an excuse for poor behavior. Avoid personal attacks and always treat others with respect.

- **Use professional language.**  
  Harassment, exclusionary behavior, or offensive language are not tolerated. This includes:
  - Threats or violent language
  - Discriminatory jokes or slurs
  - Posting explicit or violent material
  - Doxing (sharing personal information)
  - Personal insults, especially racist or sexist
  - Unwelcome sexual attention
  - Encouraging or advocating any of the above

- **Assume good intentions.**  
  Misunderstandings happen, especially in a global, digital community. Ask for clarification before assuming negative intent.

- **Resolve disagreements constructively.**  
  Seek to understand different perspectives and work together to find solutions. Mistakes happen—focus on learning and improvement.

---

## Reporting and Support

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- **Talk directly** with the person involved, if you feel comfortable.
- **Contact HOT’s Community Working Group:**  
  [community@hotosm.org](mailto:community@hotosm.org)
- **Contact a HOT Board Member:**  
  [hotosm.org/board](https://www.hotosm.org/board)
- **For formal complaints:**  
  Email [complaints@hotosm.org](mailto:complaints@hotosm.org) with details. All complaints are confidential.  
  See the [full complaint process](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xb-SPADtSbgwl6mAgglHMPHpknt-E7lKRoIcSbW431A/edit).
